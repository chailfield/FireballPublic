Privacy Policy for Fireball Drinking Game

Last updated: 2026-09-07

This Privacy Policy explains how Fireball Drinking Game (the "App") handles information.

1. Data we collect

The App does not require account sign-in and does not intentionally collect personal information such as name, email address, or phone number.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores gameplay data locally on your device (for example, player names entered in-game, settings, and scores) to provide app functionality. This local data is not shared by the App with the developer.

3. In-app purchases

The App offers an optional digital purchase ("Unlimited Play") through Google Play Billing. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive your full payment card details.

4. Network usage and third-party services

The App may access the internet to load map imagery and place information. These services may process technical data such as IP address and device/network metadata as part of normal content delivery.

5. Children

The App is a drinking game intended for adults and is not directed to children.

6. Data sharing

The App does not sell your personal information.

7. Your choices

You can stop all collection of app-related data by uninstalling the App. You can clear locally stored app data using your device settings.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
